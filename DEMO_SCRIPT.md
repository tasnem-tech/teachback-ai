# 2-minute demo script

**0:00–0:15 — Problem**
"Students can reread a page five times and still not know whether they actually understand it. TeachBack AI tests understanding by asking one simple question: can you explain it in your own words?"

**0:15–0:35 — Input + concept extraction**
Paste the sample machine-learning notes. Show the extracted concept map.
"The app takes the learner's own material and automatically extracts the important concepts using NLP."

**0:35–1:05 — Teach-back**
Choose `overfitting` or `supervised learning`. Give a deliberately incomplete explanation.
"Instead of asking a multiple-choice question, TeachBack asks me to teach the idea back. Sentence-BERT compares the meaning of my explanation with the most relevant source context."
Click **Analyse my understanding**.

**1:05–1:25 — Feedback**
Show score and missing ideas.
"It doesn't just say wrong. It shows which important ideas my explanation missed and gives me a focused next step. The score is a revision signal, not an official grade."

**1:25–1:42 — Adaptive practice**
Open Adaptive Practice.
"Because I struggled here, practice is automatically prioritised toward that weak concept."

**1:42–1:55 — Dashboard**
Open Mastery Dashboard.
"As I practise, TeachBack builds a mastery map and tells me what to revise next."

**1:55–2:00 — Close**
"TeachBack AI turns passive notes into an active, personalised learning loop: explain, detect gaps, practise, master."
